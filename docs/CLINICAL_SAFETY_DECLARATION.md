## 1. Ethical & Medical Target Definitions

In strict accordance with biomedical research ethics and machine learning validation guidelines:

1. **Prediction Target Scope:**
   - The primary dataset, **PhysioNet Clinical Time Series Datasets for Trajectory Flow Matching Evaluation (eICU Database v2.0)**, represents intensive care unit (ICU) patients at risk for acute cardiac collapse.
   - The documented outcome fields in this cohort are:
     - `ICU_MORT`: Intensive Care Unit Mortality (Acute Deterioration Outcome)
     - `HOSP_MORT`: In-Hospital Mortality
     - `MI`: Acute Myocardial Infarction
   - **Crucial Medical Clarification:** The dataset does **not** provide an explicit timestamped cardiac arrest event label. Therefore, our models are explicitly evaluated and described as **Cardiac Risk & Acute Physiological Deterioration Early-Warning Systems**.
   - We do not represent Myocardial Infarction or In-Hospital Mortality as direct cardiac arrest events.
   - We do not claim an algorithm predicts sudden cardiac arrest unless explicit event markers exist.

2. **PhysioNet Credentialing & DUA Requirements:**
   - Raw clinical databases (eICU Collaborative Research Database v2.0 and MIMIC-IV-Ext-MDS-ED) require completion of CITI "Data or Specimens Only Research" training and a formal PhysioNet Data Use Agreement (DUA).
   - Our system architecture includes:
     - Automatic detection of raw credentialed files in `data/raw/`.
     - Secure handling that never exposes protected health information (PHI) or patient identifiers.
     - Fully reproducible, mathematically verified clinical benchmark generators clearly tagged as `CLINICAL_BENCHMARK_DEMO` to guarantee offline reproducibility without compromising PhysioNet access restrictions.

3. **Clinical Decision Support Framing:**
   - The outputs of this software prototype represent empirical probabilities of physiological decompensation.
   - All risk categories (Low Risk <30%, Moderate Risk 30-65%, High Risk >=65%) are computational early-warning indicators intended to assist bedside monitoring, not autonomous diagnostic decisions.
   - This prototype must never replace physician judgment, clinical laboratory diagnostics, or formal resuscitation protocols.
